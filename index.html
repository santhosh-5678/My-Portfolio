Hi, I’m Santhosh Kumar Naik, a 2026 B.Tech graduate in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning from Hyderabad. I got into tech because I love figuring out how things work behind the screen, and that curiosity pulled me toward backend development and AI. I’m still early in my career, but I’m always learning, and I enjoy turning new ideas into something that actually works.

Most of what I know, I learned by building and breaking things. I usually start small, get a basic version running, and then test it with messy, real-world inputs to see where it fails. Then I go back, find the root cause, and fix it properly instead of patching it. I like to think ahead about errors, retries, and bad data, and I avoid repeating work by saving results in a database. Feedback from mentors and code reviews has helped me improve a lot, and I try to apply it every time.
